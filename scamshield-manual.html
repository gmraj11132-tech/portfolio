1. Project Overview & The Global Cyber Fraud Crisis

Globally, over $10.3 Billion is lost annually to social engineering and digital fraud. Attackers exploit psychological panic using fake KYC expiry notices, spoofed bank URLs, fake UPI payment confirmations, and coercive urgency. Existing spam filters offer opaque binary classifications that fail to educate users or verify credentials safely.

Flaws in Traditional Threat Filters

  • Opaque Classifications: Black-box labels ("Spam") without explaining what words or URLs triggered the flag.
  • High AI Hallucinations: Standard LLMs making authoritative assumptions without citing literal text evidence.
  • Privacy Exploitation: Online checkers uploading sensitive personal messages and financial screenshots to external databases.
  • Fragile Cloud Dependency: Complete failure to evaluate threats when network connectivity drops.

The ScamShield AI Engineering Solution

  • Verbatim Evidence Flagging: Quotes the exact phrases that triggered risk tags: [Urgency], [Credential], [Financial].
  • Hybrid Dual-Engine: Weighted mean (70% Gemini 2.5 Flash + 30% Deterministic regex heuristic rules).
  • In-Browser OCR: Tesseract.js Web Worker extracts image text 100% locally with zero server upload.
  • Safe Verification Protocol: Outlines precise out-of-band verification steps and a strict DO NOT checklist.

Core Viva Defense Concept: Non-Authoritative Risk Modeling

In cybersecurity, definitive statements like "This is guaranteed to be safe" can lead to liability and user harm. ScamShield AI implements Non-Authoritative Risk Modeling: it highlights mathematical signals, verbatim coercive triggers, and historical threat patterns. Even with low-risk scores, the platform advises safe out-of-band verification steps rather than unconditional trust.

6. Technical Defense & Viva Quick Reference

Q1: Why combine Neural AI with a Deterministic Rule Engine?

Pure LLMs can hallucinate false threats or miss known malicious regex signatures. Combining 70% neural reasoning with 30% deterministic heuristics grounds the score in verifiable regex patterns while handling creative social engineering.

Q2: How is user data kept confidential during analysis?

OCR extraction occurs 100% locally in the browser via Tesseract.js Web Workers. In deterministic mode, zero packets leave the user's browser. When Gemini is queried, only the ephemeral prompt is sent without permanent backend logging.

Q3: How does the system avoid false alarms on legitimate bank OTPs?

The signal engine checks for coercive elements. Legitimate bank OTPs state "Do not share this code" (protective intent), whereas phishing messages command "Submit OTP to prevent account suspension within 10 minutes" (coercive intent).

Q4: Why run OCR on the client instead of a backend server?

Client-side OCR using Tesseract.js ensures zero transmission of sensitive financial screenshots (like payment slips or account statements) over the network. It eliminates server infrastructure costs and guarantees user privacy.
